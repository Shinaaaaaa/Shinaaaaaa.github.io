Hi! I am Linxi Jiang, a second-year PhD student at The Ohio State University (OSU), advised by Professor Zhiqiang Lin in the SecLab. My research focuses on the security of agent systems, with a broader interest in system and software security. I am particularly interested in exploring how emerging intelligent agents interact with complex environments and how to make these systems more secure and trustworthy.

I received my Bachelor's degree from the ACM Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). During my undergraduate years, I had the opportunity to work with Associate Professor Guoxing Chen at the NSEC Lab, where I gained valuable experience in security research.
